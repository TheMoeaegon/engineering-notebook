## Structure

- Notes are grouped by topic in folders (for example `python-reference/` and `js-node-reference/`).
- Screenshots, diagrams, and videos live in [`media/`](./media).
- Notes may mix course material with my own explanations and examples; sources are cited at the top where relevant.

## Why keep a notebook?

Writing an explanation forces me to find the gaps in my understanding. If I can't explain it simply, I probably don't understand it yet — and these notes are where that explanation gets written down and corrected over time.
